# Product Manifesto — v0.1

**Working product:** Jinn (final brand undecided)  
**Question:** *Who do you want to be?*  
**Promise:** *Three wishes. Real work.*

We are not building a general-purpose AI assistant or a motivational coach. We are building a low-friction consumer experience that takes a human aspiration and completes a bounded, useful piece of work toward it.

## Principles

1. **Compete on effort, not intelligence.** A human shouldn't need to know how to prompt or operate agents to obtain a real-world outcome.
2. **Actions before advice.** Research, make, prepare, publish (with approval), monitor; do not disguise suggestions as completed tasks.
3. **Open aspiration, controlled capabilities.** People may wish for anything. We only promise what our skill catalog can reliably deliver.
4. **Reveal value before asking questions.** Keep onboarding to one context question at a time; show the capability being unlocked.
5. **Truthful receipts.** FOUND, CREATED, PREPARED, EXECUTED, VERIFIED and FAILED must mean different things. Never fabricate action.
6. **Consent over convenience.** No silent social profiling by email/name; no external sends, publications or payments without exact authority.
7. **Personal by choice, private by default.** Respect identity and sensitive goals; don't push broad account connections.
8. **Selective, economical intelligence.** Use code, cached public research and small models where possible; no always-on LLM loops.
9. **Genuinely useful free entry.** No subscription at launch; optional paid bounded work later. No manipulative affiliate incentives.
10. **One impressive vertical slice over a feature catalog.** Build the outcome first, architecture as demanded.

## Commercial experiment

Founder's initial ambition: CAD $400–$500/month additional income, potential to scale. Available 1–3 months for initial build/validation. Cap early discretionary spend; aim for under CAD $2,000 until meaningful evidence. Primary measures: first-use action completion, useful work acceptance, meaningful day-7/day-14 return, cost per accepted receipt and unprompted sharing.

## First-use aspiration

A minimal screen asks, "Who do you want to be?" A Jinn character offers concrete available deeds, asks the minimum relevant question, then performs selected work, with a verified receipt. The Jinn is charming in playful contexts, respectful and discreet in personal ones.

**Not a product claim yet:** three free wishes are a pricing experiment, not a final quota. Failed executions should not consume a wish; terms must be defined after actual mission costs are known.
